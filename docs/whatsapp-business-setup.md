3. If already using regular WhatsApp on this number, you'll be prompted to migrate — accept the migration to keep your chat history
4. Verify via SMS or call

> **Important:** You cannot run WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business on the same number simultaneously. Migration is one-way.

## 6. WhatsApp Channel Setup

WhatsApp Channels allow broadcast updates to followers without seeing their numbers.

### Create a Channel
1. Go to **Updates** tab → **Channels**
2. Tap **+** → **Create channel**
3. Set channel name: **Farwa Beauty Salon**
4. Add the salon logo as channel icon
5. Write channel description:
```
Official channel of Farwa Beauty Salon, PECHS Block 2, Karachi. Get updates on offers, new services, bridal season specials & beauty tips! 🌸💕
```

### Content Strategy for Channel
Post 2–3 times per week:
| Day | Content Type | Example |
|-----|-------------|---------|
| Monday | Beauty Tip | "3 steps to prep your skin before a facial..." |
| Wednesday | Offer / Promo | "This week only: 20% off all facials!" |
| Friday | Showcase | Before/after photo or bridal transformation |

### Promote the Channel
- Share channel link in the greeting message
- Add "Follow our WhatsApp Channel" to receipts and cards
- Share in Google Posts and Instagram Stories
- Add to website footer
