## 6. Content Do's and Don'ts

### Do ✅
- Show real results (before/after with consent)
- Use natural lighting for photos
- Post consistently (minimum 3x/week on Instagram)
- Engage with comments within 1 hour
- Use location tags (PECHS Block 2, Karachi)
- Share client testimonials
- Show the salon environment (clean, welcoming)
- Use trending audio on Reels/TikTok

### Don't ❌
- Post blurry or poorly lit photos
- Use stock photos instead of real work
- Go silent for more than 3 days
- Ignore DMs or comments
- Post without hashtags
- Bash competitors
- Over-filter transformation photos (breaks trust)
- Share client photos without permission
